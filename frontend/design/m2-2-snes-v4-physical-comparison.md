- Authentic NTSC-U/C Super Mario World cartridge photography: https://www.ebay.com/itm/155342209898
- Wikimedia Commons public-domain SNES and Super Famicom cartridge photography by Evan-Amos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SNES-SFAM-Cartridges.jpg
- North American cartridge bottom photograph: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:North_American_SNES_cartridge_bottom.jpg
- Nintendo USD343833S design patent: https://patents.google.com/patent/USD343833S/en
- Laser Design physical scan listing: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/super-mario-world-game-cartridge-a102d3e7fe5c4770912a56e69b04898a

No vertices, texture pixels, label artwork or photograph pixels are copied into the generated asset. The visual review changed only original RetroLife CAD parameters and boolean features.

The four active PNG views are direct renders of the committed v4 CAD tessellation. They are the primary mobile review medium.
