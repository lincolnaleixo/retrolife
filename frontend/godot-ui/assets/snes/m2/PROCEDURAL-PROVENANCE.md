# M2 procedural asset provenance

## Production source

The production North American SNES-style cartridge is original parametric RetroLife geometry implemented in `frontend/godot-ui/scripts/snes_na_cartridge.gd` for direct Godot generation.

The procedural source is released under CC0-1.0.

The implementation contains no copied third-party mesh, Nintendo logo, game-specific label, console model, ROM, or proprietary texture.

## Open reference review

The downloadable `Game cartridge` by Pavel Kutejnikov on OpenGameArt was reviewed under CC-BY 4.0. It is described as SNES-inspired and contains only 216 triangles. It was considered useful as an openly licensed blockout reference, but its geometry and textures are not redistributed, embedded, or copied into RetroLife.

## Forbidden sourcing

M2 must not incorporate assets that are:

- paid or subscription-only;
- locked behind a commercial account;
- Editorial use only;
- NonCommercial;
- NoDerivatives;
- missing source files needed for modification;
- distributed under an unclear or incompatible license.

Future geometry changes must remain original CC0 work or document a compatible open-license source and attribution record before merging.
