# Provenance

Asset `retrolife.snes.na-cartridge.m2.2.front.v4` is generated by `scripts/generate-m2-2-snes-front.py` from the public M2.1 dimensional contract.

The source builds a CadQuery/Open CASCADE B-rep and exports canonical STEP, deterministic STL and OBJ files. The mobile PNGs are rendered from the generated tessellation.

External references are comparison-only. No third-party vertices, textures, game artwork, photographs, ROMs or console geometry are embedded.

Physical caliper calibration and final owner approval remain incomplete.
